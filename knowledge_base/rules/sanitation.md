# Public sanitation (demo rule)

> DEMO CIVIC RULE for the fictional Sample Municipal Corporation. Not a real government rule.

Dirty or unusable public toilets, unclean public places, bad smell, dead animals or
mosquito breeding in public areas are **sanitation** grievances, handled by the Sanitation
Department (`DEPT-SANITATION`). Uncollected garbage is a separate garbage collection grievance.

## Details a complaint should include

- Which public place is affected, with street or locality

## Service timeline

Action within 2 days (`SLA-SANITATION`). Reference data only.
