# Potholes and damaged roads (demo rule)

> DEMO CIVIC RULE for the fictional Sample Municipal Corporation. Not a real government rule.

Potholes, cracked or broken road surfaces and caved-in stretches are **pothole and road
damage** grievances, handled by the Roads Maintenance Department (`DEPT-ROADS`).

## Details a complaint should include

- Road name and a nearby landmark
- How big or dangerous the damage is (optional)

## Service timeline

Repair within 7 days (`SLA-ROAD-DAMAGE`). Reference data only.
