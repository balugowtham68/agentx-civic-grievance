# Broken public structures (demo rule)

> DEMO CIVIC RULE for the fictional Sample Municipal Corporation. Not a real government rule.

Broken public benches, railings, footpaths, bus shelters, park gates and similar public
structures are **broken civic infrastructure** grievances, handled by the Civic Infrastructure
Maintenance Department (`DEPT-PUBLIC-WORKS`). Potholes belong to road damage and street lights
to streetlight maintenance.

## Details a complaint should include

- Which structure is broken, and where

## Service timeline

Repair within 7 days (`SLA-BROKEN-INFRASTRUCTURE`). Reference data only.
