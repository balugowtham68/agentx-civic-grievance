# Blocked drains and sewage overflow (demo rule)

> DEMO CIVIC RULE for the fictional Sample Municipal Corporation. Not a real government rule.

A blocked or overflowing storm drain, an open or overflowing sewer or manhole, or sewage
standing on the road is a **drainage and sewerage** grievance, handled by the Drainage & Public
Works Department (`DEPT-DRAINAGE`).

## Details a complaint should include

- Street, locality or ward, and the nearest landmark
- Whether sewage is overflowing onto the road (optional)

## Service timeline

Clearing within 3 days (`SLA-DRAINAGE`). Reference data only.
