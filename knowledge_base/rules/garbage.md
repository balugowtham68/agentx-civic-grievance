# Uncollected garbage (demo rule)

> DEMO CIVIC RULE for the fictional Sample Municipal Corporation. Not a real government rule.

Household or street garbage that has not been picked up on the scheduled day, an overflowing
community bin, or waste dumped in a public place is a **garbage collection** grievance. It is
handled by the Sanitation Department (`DEPT-SANITATION`) of the ward where the garbage is.

## Details a complaint should include

- Street, locality or ward, so the collection vehicle can be sent
- How many days collection has been missed (optional)
- Type of waste, e.g. household or construction (optional)

## Service timeline

Collection within 2 days (`SLA-GARBAGE`). Reference data only.
